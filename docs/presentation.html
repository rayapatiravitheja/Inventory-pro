Industry Gap & Motivation

The Real-World Supply Chain Problem

❌

Single-Shop Silos

Traditional retail IMS treats stock as a single global number without granular warehouse bin coordinates or regional distribution routing.

⚠️

Concurrency & Overselling

Concurrent customer shopping sessions corrupt stock balances without atomic database locking ($gte guards and Mongoose ACID transactions).

🔄

Manual Return & Refund Delays

Customer return workflows (RMA) lack automated gateway refund disbursement and direct facility restocking into WMS bins.

🚫

Cloud SMTP Firewall Blocking

Modern cloud hosts (Render, AWS) block outbound SMTP ports (25, 465, 587) to prevent spam, causing order emails to silently drop or hang.

🛡️ Server-Side Price Authority

Never trust client cart payload. When placing an order, Express retrieves product unit prices directly from MongoDB Atlas to calculate item subtotals, GST (18%), and final payable amounts.

subTotal = DB.selling_price * qty;
gst = Math.round(subTotal * 0.18 * 100) / 100;
amountInPaise = Math.round((subTotal + gst) * 100);
